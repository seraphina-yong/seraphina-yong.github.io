Content Assignment 3 - Data Analytics. Deadline - Dec 16, 2017; 23:59

In this assignment, you will explore and perform statistical analyses on CSC Lab's MCGD Dataset http://mcgd.csclab.tw/index.html, which stands for "multidimensionally codified gesture dataset for interpersonal communication research".

The data were collected from a group brainstorming study with 36 pairs of participants (i.e., 72 individuals).  Each pair performed a series of group idea generation sessions on given brainstorming tasks ("extra eye", "extra thumb", "extra wing" tasks, see the front page of the website for an explanation/illustration of the tasks, http://mcgd.csclab.tw/index.html) There are 5 different types of data. All data were collected in a lab study. Verbal data, ideas and gestures performed by each participants were collected.
 
Refer to the website and the documents http://mcgd.csclab.tw/publication.html for more details about the construction of the dataset.

Now, please explore at least the following two sets of data with JMP in your assignment: Verbal Data http://mcgd.csclab.tw/data_verbal.html and Idea Count http://mcgd.csclab.tw/data_idea.html

Pre-process the data in ways that you believe are necessary, form research questions, explore the data using statistical methods and visualizations, and then write-up the results of your analyses in an appropriate format.

Note: This is an open-ended assignment, please use your creativity and knowledge to work on this assignment. We hope to learn from you what you found from the dataset!
