For the first assignment, please write down 3 pages project description (in English, 800-1000 words expected) including an introduction to the background and what you plan to do and a list of research questions. It may concerns with your following assignments in this semester.

There's no strict format about how this proposal should look like, but normally it should have the following sections:

- Introduction, which provides an overview of what's the motivation of your work, why is it important, what's your approach, and what are the research questions you're going to investigate and answer etc. Again there's some flexibility about what to write in an introduction, which also depends on topics and communities. 

- Literature review or "background" or "related work". This is more about what are the prior work related to the current research proposal. In a paper that's more technology-oriented, this section is often titled "related work", and the content would be more around similar technologies that have been developed and tested previously. In work that involve user research and design, this section is typically titled as "background", which reviews and summarizes theories and empirical work that serve as a foundation of the current research. They shouldn't be just "related". It's the best that they're logically essential.

- Research questions or "hypotheses". What are interesting for us to study? What's unknown? Frame your research questions appropriately so that they are (1) important, and (2) answerable by using research methods.

- Approach. Give some initial, maybe non-educated thoughts on how you may answer your research questions. You should already have some rough ideas on research methods (experiments, interviews, surveys etc.). Even what you know may lack details or could be inaccurate. Try to write down your initial thoughts on what you may do to collect and analyze data. 

- References. Important to cite as in all research fields.
