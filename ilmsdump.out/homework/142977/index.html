Assignment 2- Experimental Design 

According to your research topic and questions, propose a full experiment design (in special cases, could be qualitative study design) that can effectively answer your research questions with data. Write a persuasive document to motivate your experiment and explain the rationale of your experimental design. Consider the following issues in your write-up:

- What are the key dependent variables (measurements) of your study? Why?
- What are the independent variables (factors) that you're manipulating and looking at? Why?
- Are you proposing a with-in subject design or a between-subject design or a mix? How many participants do you need? Justify your experiment design with consideration of costs and benefits of all possible aspects.
- What are your hypotheses or predictions of the results?
- How would you visualize your data in the report? Illustrate your ideas with mock-up visualizations.
- Pilot your experimental design with a small data set if possible.

It would be very helpful if you can collect a small set of data to show that your experimental design is practical and runnable. 

There's no page limitation for your report.
